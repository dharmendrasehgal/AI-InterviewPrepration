## KPI Framework

KPIs are organized into three layers:
- **North Star Metric** — single number that captures platform value delivery
- **Primary KPIs** — directly measure each business objective
- **Secondary KPIs** — leading indicators and health signals

---

## North Star Metric

| Metric | Definition | Target (12-month) |
|--------|-----------|-------------------|
| **Weekly Active Practicing Candidates (WAPC)** | Number of unique candidates who complete at least 1 AI or human-led mock session in a rolling 7-day window | 5,000 WAPC by end of Phase 2 |

_Rationale: WAPC measures active engagement in practice (not just logins), which directly predicts the platform's core value — improved interview readiness._

---

## Primary KPIs (Business Objective Alignment)

### KPI-01: Candidate Confidence Improvement
_Aligned to: BO-01_

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Definition | % of candidates who rate their interview confidence as "improved" or "significantly improved" in a post-session survey (shown after session 3+) |
| Measurement | In-app survey: 3-question Likert scale (1–5) shown after 3rd completed session |
| Target | ≥ 70% of surveyed candidates report confidence improvement |
| Frequency | Measured monthly; reported quarterly |
| Owner | Product Manager |

---

### KPI-02: AI Score Improvement Rate
_Aligned to: BO-02_

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Definition | Average change in composite AI Clarity Score between a candidate's first session and their fifth session |
| Measurement | `(avg_score_session_5 - avg_score_session_1) / avg_score_session_1 × 100%` per cohort |
| Target | ≥ 15% average improvement from session 1 to session 5 |
| Frequency | Measured weekly; reported monthly |
| Owner | Data / Engineering |

---

### KPI-03: Content Library Scale
_Aligned to: BO-03_

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Definition | Total number of published, unique questions in the Question Bank |
| Measurement | Database count of questions with `status = 'published'` |
| Target | ≥ 200 questions at Phase 1 GA; ≥ 500 by end of Phase 2; ≥ 1,000 by end of Phase 3 |
| Frequency | Reported at each sprint review |
| Owner | Content Team / Admin |
| Sub-metrics | Questions per category (Behavioral / Technical / Situational / Role-Specific); questions per industry |

---

### KPI-04: Expert Marketplace Scale
_Aligned to: BO-04_

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Definition | Number of verified, active expert profiles available for booking |
| Measurement | Count of expert accounts with `status = 'approved'` and at least 1 available slot in the next 7 days |
| Target | ≥ 50 active experts at Phase 2 launch |
| Frequency | Reported weekly from Phase 2 start |
| Owner | Operations / Product |
| Sub-metrics | Experts by industry; average expert availability (slots per week); expert profile completion rate |

---

### KPI-05: 30-Day Candidate Retention
_Aligned to: BO-05_

| Field | Detail |
|-------|--------|
| Definition | % of newly registered candidates (per cohort) who complete ≥ 3 practice sessions within their first 30 days |
| Measurement | `count(candidates with ≥3 sessions in days 1–30) / count(all candidates registered in cohort) × 100%` |
| Target | ≥ 40% retention rate |
| Frequency | Measured per weekly registration cohort; reported monthly |
| Owner | Product Manager |

## Secondary KPIs (Health & Leading Indicators)

### Acquisition

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| New Registrations (Weekly) | Unique new accounts created per week | Growth trend; absolute target TBD after Beta |
| Registration Completion Rate | % of users who complete profile setup after registering | ≥ 60% |
| Verification Email Click-Through | % of verification emails clicked within 24 hours | ≥ 75% |

---

### Activation

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Time to First Session | Time from registration to first completed AI mock session | Median ≤ 48 hours |
| Session Start Rate | % of registered candidates who start at least 1 AI session | ≥ 50% within 7 days of registration |
| Profile Completion Rate | % of registered candidates who complete profile setup (resume + target role) | ≥ 70% |

---

### Engagement

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Sessions per Active User (Weekly) | Average AI sessions completed per WAPC per week | ≥ 1.5 |
| Question Bank Engagement | % of sessions preceded by Question Bank browsing in the same session | ≥ 30% |
| Bookmark Rate | % of active candidates with ≥ 5 bookmarked questions | ≥ 25% |
| Feedback Archive Open Rate | % of candidates who open their Feedback Archive within 24 hours of session completion | ≥ 60% |

---

### Quality & Performance

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| STT Transcription Accuracy | % of sessions where candidate reports transcript is "accurate" (in-session feedback) | ≥ 85% |
| Score Report Delivery Time | p95 time from session end to score report available | ≤ 60 seconds |
| Session Recording Success Rate | % of sessions where recording is successfully stored and playable | ≥ 99% |
| Page Load Time (TTI) | Time to Interactive on Question Bank and Dashboard pages | ≤ 2 seconds (p75) |
| Platform Uptime | Monthly uptime | ≥ 99.5% |

---

### Expert Marketplace (Phase 2)

| Metric | Definition | Target |
|--------|-----------|--------|
| Booking Conversion Rate | % of expert profile views that result in a booking | ≥ 10% |
| Session No-Show Rate | % of booked sessions where one party does not join | ≤ 5% |
| Rubric Completion Rate | % of live sessions where expert submits rubric within 24 hours | ≥ 80% |
| Candidate Satisfaction (Post-Live Session) | Average rating (1–5) given by candidate after a live session | ≥ 4.2 |

## KPI Dashboard Requirements

The following metrics must be surfaced in real-time or near-real-time for the Admin and Product team:

| Dashboard View | Metrics |
|---------------|---------|
| **Executive Summary** | WAPC, 30-day retention, new registrations (weekly trend), AI score improvement rate |
| **Content Health** | Total published questions, questions per category, content coverage gaps |
| **Technical Health** | Uptime, error rate, STT accuracy, score report delivery time |
| **Expert Marketplace** (Phase 2) | Active experts, booking conversion rate, rubric completion rate, candidate satisfaction |

> Note for `senior_architect_agent`: Analytics pipeline must support at minimum daily batch reporting for retention and improvement KPIs. WAPC and technical health metrics require near-real-time streaming aggregation.
