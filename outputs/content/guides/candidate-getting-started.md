# Getting Started — Candidates
## Interview Preparation Platform

> Version: 1.0 | Audience: Job Seekers & Career Changers

---

## Welcome

This guide walks you through everything you need to start practising, improving, and tracking your interview readiness — from creating your account to reviewing your first AI score report.

---

## Step 1: Create Your Account

1. Go to the platform homepage and click **Sign up free**.
2. Choose **Candidate** as your role.
3. Enter your full name, email address, and a password (minimum 8 characters, at least one number and one uppercase letter).
4. Alternatively, click **Continue with Google** to register in one click.
5. Check your inbox for a verification email and click the link inside. The link expires after 24 hours.

> **Tip:** If you don't see the email, check your spam folder or click **Resend verification** on the login page.

---

## Step 2: Set Up Your Profile

Your profile helps the platform personalise your practice questions. The more detail you provide, the more targeted your sessions will be.

**From your dashboard, click Profile → Set up my profile:**

1. **Upload your resume** — Drag and drop a PDF or DOCX file (max 5 MB). The platform will extract your current role, skills, and experience automatically.
2. **Confirm your details** — Review the extracted information and edit anything that looks incorrect.
3. **Select your interview track:**
   - **General Career** — Broad behavioural and situational questions for any industry
   - **Software Engineering** — Technical interviews, system design, and coding-focused questions
   - **Medical / Residency** — NRMP, medical school, and specialty interview preparation
4. **Choose your career level** — Entry, Mid, Senior, or Executive.
5. Click **Save profile** to continue.

---

## Step 3: Explore the Question Bank

The Question Bank contains hundreds of expert-vetted questions with structured sample answers.

**How to browse:**
- Use the **search bar** at the top of any page to find questions by keyword (e.g., "leadership", "system design").
- Use **filters** on the left to narrow by type (Behavioural, Technical, Situational), industry, career level, or topic tag.
- Click any question to read the full text and reveal the sample answer (click **Show answer**).

**Save questions for later:**
- Click the **bookmark icon** on any question card to add it to your personal practice list.
- Find all your bookmarked questions under **Dashboard → My Practice List**.

---

## Step 4: Run an AI Mock Interview

An AI Mock Interview simulates a real interview — it asks you questions, records your responses, and delivers a scored feedback report within 60 seconds.

### Before you start
Make sure you are in a quiet space with your camera and microphone available. The platform will check your browser permissions.

### Starting a session
1. From your dashboard, click **Start AI Mock Interview**.
2. A **recording consent dialog** will appear. Read the terms, check the box to confirm you agree, and click **Accept & Start Session**.
   - Declining cancels the session — no recording will be made.
3. The platform checks your camera and microphone. If access is blocked, follow the on-screen instructions to grant permission in your browser settings.
4. Your first question appears on screen. Read it, take a moment to think (the STAR method works well — Situation, Task, Action, Result), then click **Start recording** when you are ready to answer.
5. Answer naturally. Click **Stop recording** when you finish.
6. The platform loads the next question. Repeat until all questions are answered.
7. Click **Submit session** — your recording is uploaded and analysed automatically.

### What happens next
Your AI Score Report appears within 60 seconds. It is also saved permanently in your **Feedback Archive**.

---

## Step 5: Review Your Score Report

Your score report breaks down your performance across every question:

| Metric | What it means |
|--------|--------------|
| **Clarity Score** | An overall 0–100 score combining all metrics |
| **Speech Rate (WPM)** | Words per minute — ideal range is 130–160 WPM |
| **Filler Words** | Count of "um", "uh", "like", "you know" — aim for < 2% of your total words |
| **Keyword Relevance** | How closely your answer matched the expected key concepts |

Each metric includes a **personalised improvement tip** — for example: *"Your speech rate was 185 WPM — try slowing down slightly for clarity."*

At the bottom of the report, click **Replay recording** to watch your session back and review your non-verbal delivery alongside your score.

---

## Step 6: Track Your Progress

Your **Dashboard** shows your improvement over time:

- **Sessions Completed** — total AI and live sessions
- **Average Score** — your composite score averaged across all sessions
- **Score Trend** — a chart showing your last 10 session scores

Aim to complete at least 3 sessions per week. Most candidates see a measurable score improvement after 5 sessions.

---

## Step 7: Access Your Feedback Archive

Every session — including recordings, transcripts, and scores — is saved in your **Feedback Archive**.

**From the dashboard, click Feedback Archive:**
- Filter sessions by type (AI or Live), date range, or score range.
- Click any session to replay the recording and review notes.
- Click **Download transcript (PDF)** to save a text copy of your responses.

> **Privacy note:** Recordings are automatically deleted after 90 days. You can delete any recording manually at any time from the Archive.
