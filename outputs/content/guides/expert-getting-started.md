# Getting Started — Experts & Mentors
## Interview Preparation Platform

> Version: 1.0 | Audience: Recruiters, Coaches, and Industry Professionals

---

## Welcome

Thank you for joining as an Expert. This guide covers how to set up your profile, manage your availability, conduct live mock interview sessions, and submit feedback that helps candidates grow.

---

## Step 1: Register as an Expert

1. Go to the platform homepage and click **Sign up free**.
2. Select **Expert / Mentor** as your role.
3. Enter your full name, email, and password — or use **Continue with Google**.
4. Verify your email address via the link sent to your inbox.

> Your profile will not be visible to candidates until it is reviewed and approved by the platform team (usually within 2 business days).

---

## Step 2: Complete Your Expert Profile

A complete, detailed profile builds candidate trust and increases your booking rate.

**From your dashboard, click Profile → Complete my expert profile:**

1. **Bio** — Write 2–4 sentences describing your background, areas of expertise, and what candidates can expect from a session with you. Be specific: mention industries, roles, and any specialties.
2. **Specialties** — Select all that apply: Software Engineering, Product Management, Medical / Residency, Finance, General Career, and more.
3. **Years of experience** — Enter your total years in your field.
4. **LinkedIn URL** — Required for verification. Your public profile must be accessible.
5. Click **Submit for review**.

### What happens after submission
- The platform team reviews your profile, typically within 2 business days.
- You receive an email notification when approved (or if more information is needed).
- Once approved, your profile appears in the Expert Marketplace and candidates can book sessions.

---

## Step 3: Set Your Availability

Candidates can only book sessions during the time slots you publish.

**From your dashboard, click Availability:**

1. Click on any time slot in the calendar to mark it as available.
2. Select the session duration (30, 45, or 60 minutes).
3. Click **Save availability**.
4. Your open slots appear immediately in the marketplace for candidates to book.

**Tips:**
- Publish at least 5–10 slots per week to appear prominently in search results.
- Update your availability each week — expired slots with no bookings are removed automatically.
- You can block slots at any time as long as no session is booked in that slot.

---

## Step 4: Manage Bookings

When a candidate books a session with you:

1. You receive an **email confirmation** with the candidate's name, session time, and any preparation notes they have left.
2. A **reminder email** is sent 24 hours before, and again 1 hour before the session.
3. The booking appears in your **Upcoming Sessions** dashboard widget.

### Cancelling a session
- You can cancel up to **24 hours before** the scheduled start time.
- Go to **My Sessions → [Session] → Cancel session**.
- Both you and the candidate receive a cancellation notification immediately.
- Repeated last-minute cancellations may affect your marketplace ranking.

---

## Step 5: Conduct a Live Session

At the scheduled time, join from your dashboard or directly from the confirmation email link.

1. Click **Join Session** (the button activates 10 minutes before start time).
2. A **recording consent dialog** appears — both you and the candidate must accept before the call begins.
3. The video call connects directly in your browser — no software installation required.
4. Conduct the interview as you normally would. Suggested structure:
   - **5 min** — Introduce yourself and set expectations
   - **30 min** — Ask 4–6 interview questions (you may use the platform's suggested question list)
   - **10 min** — Share high-level verbal feedback and allow candidate questions
5. End the call by clicking **End Session**.

> **Technical issues?** If the video connection fails, use the backup link provided in your confirmation email. Contact support@platform.com for urgent issues during a live session.

---

## Step 6: Submit Your Evaluation

After the session ends, you have **24 hours** to submit your structured evaluation. Candidates receive a notification as soon as you submit — prompt feedback is highly valued.

**From your dashboard, click My Sessions → [Session] → Complete Evaluation:**

### Rubric scoring (rate each 1–5 stars)

| Category | What to assess |
|----------|---------------|
| **Communication** | Clarity of speech, structure of answers, use of examples |
| **Competence** | Domain knowledge, problem-solving approach, accuracy |
| **Confidence** | Pacing, tone, eye contact, composure under pressure |
| **Preparation** | Familiarity with the role/company, specific examples ready |

### Comments (optional but strongly encouraged)
Add a free-text comment for each category. Specific, actionable feedback — rather than general praise — helps candidates improve fastest.

**Example of strong feedback:**
> *"Your answer to the system design question showed good high-level thinking, but you didn't address database choice or failure modes. For FAANG interviews, always cover fault tolerance and scale estimates."*

### Overall summary
Write 2–4 sentences summarising the candidate's strongest areas and the one or two things to focus on before their next interview.

Click **Submit evaluation** — this cannot be edited after submission.

## Code of Conduct

As an Expert on the platform, you agree to:

- Arrive on time and prepared for every booked session
- Treat all candidates with respect regardless of background or experience level
- Submit evaluations within 24 hours of session completion
- Not solicit candidates for paid coaching outside the platform
- Maintain confidentiality of all candidate responses and recordings

Violations may result in removal from the marketplace.
