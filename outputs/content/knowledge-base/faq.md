# Frequently Asked Questions
## Interview Preparation Platform

> Last updated: 2026-05-26 | Audience: All users

## General

**What is the Interview Preparation Platform?**  
It is an AI-powered website that helps job seekers practise for interviews through on-demand AI mock sessions, a curated question bank, and (in Phase 2) live 1-on-1 sessions with verified expert mentors. The platform records your responses, analyses your speech, and delivers a scored feedback report so you know exactly what to improve.

**Who is it for?**  
Anyone preparing for a job interview — from students applying for their first role to senior professionals targeting executive positions. The platform has dedicated content tracks for Software Engineering, Medical / Residency, and General Career preparation.

**Is it free?**  
A free tier is available with access to the question bank and a limited number of AI mock sessions. Pricing details for premium features are available on the Pricing page.

**What do I need to get started?**  
A device with a webcam and microphone, a modern browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Edge recommended), and a stable internet connection. No software installation is required.

## Account & Profile

**How do I create an account?**  
Click **Sign up free** on the homepage. You can register with an email and password or use your Google account for instant access.

**I didn't receive my verification email — what should I do?**  
Check your spam or junk folder first. If you still don't see it, go to the login page and click **Resend verification email**. The link expires after 24 hours.

**Can I change my interview track after setting up my profile?**  
Yes. Go to **Profile → Edit profile** at any time and update your track, career level, or target industry. Your next AI mock session will use the updated settings.

**How do I upload my resume?**  
From your dashboard, go to **Profile → Set up my profile** and drag your PDF or DOCX file into the upload area (max 5 MB). The platform extracts your skills, current role, and experience automatically. You review and confirm before saving.

**Can I delete my account?**  
Yes. Go to **Profile → Account settings → Delete account**. All your personal data, recordings, and transcripts are permanently deleted within 30 days of the request.

## AI Mock Interviews

**How does the AI mock interview work?**  
The platform asks you a series of interview questions tailored to your profile. You record your answers via your webcam and microphone. After the session, the AI analyses your responses and generates a scored report covering your speech rate, filler word usage, and answer quality.

**How long is a session?**  
Sessions have 3–10 questions (you choose at the start). Each question gives you up to 2 minutes to answer (configurable between 30 seconds and 5 minutes). A typical 5-question session takes 10–15 minutes.

**Why do I have to accept a consent dialog before each session?**  
The platform records your video and audio during the session. Recording consent is required by law in many regions (including GDPR and CCPA). Accepting confirms you understand and agree to the recording. You can delete the recording at any time.

**What if I decline the consent dialog?**  
The session will not start and no recording will be made. You can restart a session at any time.

**My camera was not detected — what should I do?**  
1. Make sure your browser has camera and microphone permissions. In Chrome: click the lock icon in the address bar → Permissions → allow Camera and Microphone.
2. Make sure no other application (Zoom, Teams) is using your camera.
3. Try a different browser. Chrome and Firefox have the broadest device support.
4. If the issue persists, contact support@platform.com.

**What browsers support AI session recording?**  
Chrome 110+, Firefox 110+, and Edge 110+ have full support. Safari 16+ is supported but has some limitations with certain video formats. Internet Explorer is not supported.

## Scores & Feedback

**What does my Clarity Score mean?**  
The Clarity Score (0–100) is a composite of three metrics:
- **Keyword relevance (50%)** — how well your answer covered the expected key concepts
- **Speech pacing (25%)** — how close your rate was to the ideal 130–160 words per minute
- **Filler word usage (25%)** — how few filler words ("um", "uh", "like") appeared in your answer

A score above 70 is considered strong. Most candidates improve by 15–20 points after 5 sessions.

**What is a good speech rate?**  
130–160 words per minute (WPM) is the recommended range for clear, confident interview delivery. Below 100 WPM sounds hesitant; above 180 WPM is difficult to follow. The platform highlights your rate and flags if you are significantly outside this range.

**How quickly is my score report available?**  
The report is available within 60 seconds of submitting your session in the vast majority of cases. During periods of high demand, it may take up to 3 minutes.

**Can I retake a session on the same questions?**  
The platform selects questions adaptively each session. If you want to practise a specific question, find it in the Question Bank, bookmark it, and use it in a self-practice recording (available in Phase 2).

## Feedback Archive & Recordings

**How long are my recordings stored?**  
Recordings are retained for 90 days by default and then automatically deleted. You can delete any recording earlier from your Feedback Archive.

**Can I download my recordings?**  
Transcript PDFs are available for download. Video recordings can be replayed within the platform but cannot currently be downloaded directly (available in a future update).

**Who can see my recordings?**  
Only you. Recordings from AI mock sessions are private by default. For live sessions with an expert, both you and the expert have access to the session recording.

**Can I delete a specific recording?**  
Yes. Go to **Feedback Archive → [Session] → Delete recording**. The video file is permanently deleted immediately.

## Privacy & Security

**Is my data secure?**  
Yes. All personal data (recordings, transcripts, profile information) is encrypted at rest using AES-256 and in transit using TLS 1.3. The platform complies with GDPR and CCPA.

**Does the platform share my data with third parties?**  
No personal data or recordings are shared with third parties without your explicit consent. The platform uses Anthropic's Claude API to analyse your responses; data sent to the API is governed by a Data Processing Agreement and is not used to train Anthropic's models.

**How do I request a copy of my data (GDPR)?**  
Email privacy@platform.com with the subject line "Data access request". We will respond within 30 days with a full export of your data.

**How do I request deletion of my data (right to erasure)?**  
Go to **Profile → Account settings → Delete account**, or email privacy@platform.com. All personal data is deleted within 30 days.

## Technical

**The platform is slow on my connection — what can I do?**  
- Use a wired connection or move closer to your Wi-Fi router.
- Close other browser tabs and applications using bandwidth (video calls, streaming).
- For AI sessions, a minimum of 5 Mbps upload speed is recommended for reliable video recording.

**Can I use the platform on mobile?**  
The platform is fully responsive and works on mobile browsers. For AI mock interview sessions (which require camera and microphone), a tablet or laptop with a front-facing camera provides the best experience. A native mobile app is planned for Phase 3.

**Can I use the platform offline?**  
The Question Bank and Playbooks (Phase 2) are available offline if you install the platform as a Progressive Web App (PWA) on your device. AI mock sessions require an internet connection.
