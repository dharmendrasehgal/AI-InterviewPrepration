## What's New

We are excited to launch the Interview Preparation Platform — an AI-powered practice environment designed to help job seekers build confidence and perform their best in any interview.

### AI Mock Interviews

Practice on demand, any time. Our AI conducts adaptive mock interview sessions tailored to your target role and experience level. It asks open-ended questions, records your responses, and delivers a detailed score report in under 60 seconds.

**What you get:**
- Personalised question selection based on your resume and target role
- Video and audio recording of your responses
- Per-question breakdown: speech rate, filler word count, and keyword relevance
- An overall Clarity Score (0–100) with a session-level summary
- Personalised improvement tips for every response

### Question Bank

Browse and search a library of expert-vetted interview questions across:
- **Types:** Behavioural, Technical, Situational
- **Industries:** General Career, Software Engineering, Medical / Residency
- **Career levels:** Entry, Mid, Senior

Every question includes a sample answer written by industry professionals, with the recommended answering framework (STAR, SOAR, etc.) clearly labelled.

### Candidate Profile & Resume Parsing

Upload your resume and let the platform parse your experience, skills, and current role automatically. This personalises your AI mock sessions so questions match your background and target position.

### Progress Dashboard

Your personalised dashboard shows:
- Total sessions completed
- Average score across all sessions
- A trend chart of your last 10 scores so you can see improvement over time
- Upcoming booked sessions and pending tasks

### Feedback Archive

Every session you complete is saved in your Feedback Archive — including the recording, transcript, AI scores, and improvement tips. Replay any session, filter by date or score range, and download your transcript as a PDF.

### Search & Discovery

Find exactly what you need with full-text search across questions, articles, and playbooks. Autocomplete suggests results as you type. Filter results by type, industry, career level, and topic tag.

## Security & Privacy

- All session recordings and personal data are encrypted at rest (AES-256) and in transit (TLS 1.3).
- Recording consent is required before any session begins and logged with a timestamp.
- You control your data — delete any recording at any time from your Feedback Archive.
- Account deletion removes all personal data within 30 days.
- Full privacy policy: platform.com/privacy

## Bug Fixes & Improvements (since Beta)

- Fixed: Search results did not update when combining multiple filters simultaneously
- Fixed: Score report occasionally showed a blank improvement tip when Claude API responded slowly — now falls back to a local tip within 2 seconds
- Fixed: Camera indicator light remained on after navigating away from a session — camera and microphone are now released immediately on session end or page navigation
- Fixed: Registration form did not show an inline error for passwords shorter than 8 characters — now validated client-side before submission
- Improved: Score report now loads within 45 seconds for 95% of sessions (down from 75 seconds in Beta)
- Improved: Question Bank filter panel is now keyboard-accessible with full focus management
