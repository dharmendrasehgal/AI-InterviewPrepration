## 1. User & Account Management

### FR-01: Authentication & Registration

| ID | Functional Requirement |
|----|------------------------|
| FR-01.1 | The system shall allow users to register with email/password or Google OAuth. |
| FR-01.2 | During registration, the user shall select a role: Candidate or Expert/Mentor. Admin accounts are created internally by existing Admins. |
| FR-01.3 | The system shall send an email verification link upon registration before activating the account. |
| FR-01.4 | The system shall support password reset via email with a time-limited (1 hour) reset token. |
| FR-01.5 | The system shall enforce role-based access controls — Candidates cannot access expert rubric forms; Experts cannot access Admin dashboards. |
| FR-01.6 | Sessions shall expire after 30 days of inactivity (configurable by Admin). |

### FR-02: Candidate Profile

| ID | Functional Requirement |
|----|------------------------|
| FR-02.1 | Candidates shall be able to upload a resume (PDF, DOCX) or paste a target job description. |
| FR-02.2 | The system shall parse uploaded resumes to extract: name, current role, skills, and work history. |
| FR-02.3 | The system shall use parsed resume data and target job description to personalize the AI mock interview question set. |
| FR-02.4 | Candidates shall be able to set their target industry, career level (entry/mid/senior), and interview track (General/Software Engineering/Medical). |
| FR-02.5 | The Candidate Dashboard shall display: total sessions completed, average AI score, improvement trend graph (last 10 sessions), and pending bookings. |

### FR-03: Expert Profile

| ID | Functional Requirement |
|----|------------------------|
| FR-03.1 | Experts shall complete a profile including: name, bio, areas of expertise, years of experience, and LinkedIn URL. |
| FR-03.2 | Expert profiles shall be submitted for Admin review before appearing in the marketplace. |
| FR-03.3 | Admins shall be able to approve, reject, or request additional information from expert applicants. |
| FR-03.4 | Approved experts shall be able to set their weekly availability in a calendar interface. |

### FR-04: Admin Dashboard

| ID | Functional Requirement |
|----|------------------------|
| FR-04.1 | Admins shall have access to a user management panel listing all Candidates and Experts with status (active, suspended, pending). |
| FR-04.2 | Admins shall be able to suspend, reinstate, or delete user accounts. |
| FR-04.3 | Admins shall be able to create, edit, publish, and archive questions in the Question Bank. |
| FR-04.4 | Admins shall have access to platform analytics: total registered users, sessions per day, average AI scores, and expert utilization. |

## 2. Content & Resource Library

### FR-05: Question Bank

| ID | Functional Requirement |
|----|------------------------|
| FR-05.1 | Questions shall be categorized by: Type (Behavioral, Technical, Situational, Role-Specific), Industry, Career Level (Entry/Mid/Senior), and Topic/Tag. |
| FR-05.2 | Each question shall include: question text, difficulty rating (1–5), expected answer framework (e.g., STAR), at least one expert-vetted sample answer, and the domain tag. |
| FR-05.3 | Candidates shall be able to browse the Question Bank with multi-faceted filters. |
| FR-05.4 | Candidates shall be able to bookmark questions to a personal practice list. |
| FR-05.5 | Admins and approved Experts shall be able to submit new questions; questions require Admin approval before publishing. |

### FR-06: Search & Discovery

| ID | Functional Requirement |
|----|------------------------|
| FR-06.1 | The system shall provide a full-text search bar accessible from all content pages. |
| FR-06.2 | Search shall query questions, playbooks, article titles, and video captions. |
| FR-06.3 | Search results shall be filterable by content type, industry, career level, and topic. |
| FR-06.4 | The search input shall show autocomplete suggestions after 2 characters based on popular queries and question titles. |

### FR-07: Industry Playbooks

| ID | Functional Requirement |
|----|------------------------|
| FR-07.1 | The platform shall ship with at least 3 playbooks at launch: General Career, Software Engineering, and Medical/Residency. |
| FR-07.2 | Each playbook shall include: an overview, curated question list, recommended preparation timeline, and links to relevant video tutorials. |
| FR-07.3 | Playbook content shall be editable by Admins via a content management interface. |

### FR-08: Video Tutorials & Articles

| ID | Functional Requirement |
|----|------------------------|
| FR-08.1 | The platform shall host or embed video tutorials covering: the 5 Cs framework, STAR method, body language, and virtual interview etiquette. |
| FR-08.2 | Articles shall be displayed in a readable format with estimated read time and related question links. |
| FR-08.3 | Videos shall be captioned to meet WCAG 2.1 AA requirements. |

## 3. Interactive Practice Tools

### FR-09: AI Mock Interview (On-Demand)

| ID | Functional Requirement |
|----|------------------------|
| FR-09.1 | Candidates shall be able to start an AI mock interview session with one click from their dashboard. |
| FR-09.2 | Before the session starts, the system shall display a consent dialog explaining that audio/video will be recorded, and require explicit acceptance. |
| FR-09.3 | The AI shall ask adaptive, open-ended questions drawn from the Question Bank, personalized by the candidate's target role and past performance. |
| FR-09.4 | The session UI shall record the candidate's webcam and microphone, showing a live video preview. |
| FR-09.5 | Each question shall be displayed as text and optionally read aloud by the AI (text-to-speech). |
| FR-09.6 | Candidates shall have a configurable response time limit per question (30 seconds to 5 minutes). |
| FR-09.7 | The session shall run for 3–10 questions (configurable). Upon completion, the system shall process the recording and display the AI score report within 60 seconds. |
| FR-09.8 | The session recording shall be stored securely and linked to the Feedback Archive. |

### FR-10: Live Mock Interview (Human-Led)

| ID | Functional Requirement |
|----|------------------------|
| FR-10.1 | Candidates shall be able to browse available experts filtered by industry, specialty, and availability. |
| FR-10.2 | Candidates shall be able to book a session slot from the expert's available calendar. |
| FR-10.3 | Upon booking, both candidate and expert shall receive an email confirmation with session details and a preparation checklist. |
| FR-10.4 | The system shall send reminder notifications 24 hours and 1 hour before the session to both parties. |
| FR-10.5 | At session time, both parties shall join via a browser-based WebRTC video call (no installation required). |
| FR-10.6 | Before the call begins, both parties shall be shown a consent dialog for recording and required to accept. |
| FR-10.7 | The session recording shall be stored and linked to both the candidate's Feedback Archive and the expert's session history. |
| FR-10.8 | Candidates or experts shall be able to cancel a booking up to 24 hours in advance; both parties receive a cancellation notification. |

### FR-11: Self-Practice Record & Review

| ID | Functional Requirement |
|----|------------------------|
| FR-11.1 | Candidates shall be able to select any question from the Question Bank and record a self-practice response using their webcam and microphone. |
| FR-11.2 | Self-practice recordings shall not be shared with experts or Admins — they are private to the candidate. |
| FR-11.3 | The playback interface shall display: a waveform audio visualization, a pacing marker showing words-per-minute in 10-second intervals, and timestamp navigation. |
| FR-11.4 | Candidates shall be able to delete self-practice recordings at any time. |

### FR-12: Notifications

| ID | Functional Requirement |
|----|------------------------|
| FR-12.1 | The system shall send transactional emails for: registration verification, booking confirmation, session reminders (24h and 1h), cancellations, and feedback received. |
| FR-12.2 | The in-app notification center shall display unread notifications as a badge count on the bell icon. |
| FR-12.3 | Candidates shall be able to configure notification preferences (email on/off per category). |

## 4. Evaluation & Feedback

### FR-13: AI Scoring Engine

| ID | Functional Requirement |
|----|------------------------|
| FR-13.1 | The AI scoring engine shall analyze the STT transcript of each AI mock session response for: speech rate (words per minute), filler word count and percentage ("um", "uh", "like", "you know", "so"), keyword relevance score (0–100) against expected answer criteria. |
| FR-13.2 | Each response shall receive a composite Clarity Score (0–100) aggregated from the above metrics. |
| FR-13.3 | The AI score report shall be presented per-question and as a session-level summary. |
| FR-13.4 | The report shall include specific, actionable improvement tips per metric (e.g., "Your speech rate was 180 WPM — aim for 140–160 WPM for clarity"). |

### FR-14: Rubric-Based Expert Evaluation

| ID | Functional Requirement |
|----|------------------------|
| FR-14.1 | After a live session ends, the expert shall receive a structured evaluation form linked to that session. |
| FR-14.2 | The rubric shall include scored categories (1–5 scale): Communication Clarity, Competence / Domain Knowledge, Confidence & Presence, Preparation & Structure. |
| FR-14.3 | The expert shall be able to add free-text comments per category and an overall summary note. |
| FR-14.4 | Submitted rubric evaluations shall trigger a notification to the candidate. |
| FR-14.5 | Rubric scores shall be stored alongside the session recording in the candidate's Feedback Archive. |

### FR-15: Feedback Archive

| ID | Functional Requirement |
|----|------------------------|
| FR-15.1 | The Feedback Archive shall list all past sessions (AI and human-led) in reverse chronological order. |
| FR-15.2 | Each archive entry shall display: session date, type (AI/Human), expert name (if applicable), AI score (if applicable), rubric score summary (if applicable), and a link to replay the recording. |
| FR-15.3 | Candidates shall be able to filter the archive by session type, date range, and score range. |
| FR-15.4 | The archive shall include a progress chart showing composite score trends across all sessions. |
| FR-15.5 | Candidates shall be able to download their session transcript as a PDF. |
