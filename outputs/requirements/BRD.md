## 1. Executive Summary

The Interview Preparation Platform is an AI-powered edtech product that enables job seekers and career changers to practice, refine, and measure their interview performance through a combination of curated content and interactive practice sessions. The platform addresses a clear market gap: candidates face interviews without structured, personalized preparation tools that combine passive learning (question banks, guides) with active execution (mock interviews, real-time feedback). The platform will serve three audience segments at launch — general job seekers, software engineers, and medical/residency applicants — and expand into enterprise and institutional markets in Phase 2.

---

## 2. Business Objectives

| ID | Objective | Success Metric |
|----|-----------|----------------|
| BO-01 | Reduce candidate interview anxiety through structured practice | ≥70% of users report increased confidence in post-session survey |
| BO-02 | Deliver measurable improvement in interview performance over repeated sessions | Average AI score improvement ≥15% between session 1 and session 5 |
| BO-03 | Build a scalable content library covering at least 3 industry verticals at launch | ≥500 curated questions across Behavioral, Technical, Situational, and Role-Specific categories |
| BO-04 | Establish a trusted marketplace of verified expert mentors | ≥50 verified expert profiles available at public launch |
| BO-05 | Achieve platform retention and engagement | ≥40% of registered candidates complete at least 3 practice sessions within 30 days |

## 4. Assumptions

- MVP targets English-speaking users only (multilingual support is Phase 2).
- The platform will be web-based; native mobile apps are out of scope for v1 (a responsive PWA will be delivered).
- Monetization model will be finalized before architecture begins (see OQ-01 in `open_questions.md`).
- AI mock interview questions will be generated or curated — exact AI provider TBD (see OQ-14).
- Expert availability is self-managed by the expert via the platform's scheduling tool.

---

## 5. Constraints

- Platform must comply with GDPR and CCPA from day one.
- All video recordings require explicit dual consent before session begins.
- No candidate data may be shared with third parties without explicit consent.
- The platform must meet WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards.
- MVP delivery timeline: to be confirmed with product owner.

## 6. Business Requirements

### BR-01: User & Account Management

| ID | Business Requirement |
|----|---------------------|
| BR-01.1 | The platform must support three distinct user roles: Candidate, Expert/Mentor, and Platform Admin, each with a role-specific interface and permission set. |
| BR-01.2 | Candidates must be able to track their overall preparation progress, including completed sessions, AI scores over time, and pending tasks, from a personal dashboard. |
| BR-01.3 | Candidates must be able to upload a resume, CV, or target job description so the platform can personalize their practice question feed. |
| BR-01.4 | Platform Admins must be able to approve or reject expert profiles, moderate content, and access platform-wide analytics. |
| BR-01.5 | The platform must support secure authentication with a minimum of email/password and Google OAuth login options. |

---

### BR-02: Content & Resource Library

| ID | Business Requirement |
|----|---------------------|
| BR-02.1 | The platform must maintain a searchable question bank categorized by interview type (Behavioral, Technical, Situational, Role-Specific), industry, and career level. |
| BR-02.2 | Each question in the bank must include at least one expert-vetted sample answer with structured guidance (e.g., STAR method). |
| BR-02.3 | The platform must provide industry-specific playbooks for at least three tracks at launch: General Career, Software Engineering, and Medical/Residency. |
| BR-02.4 | The platform must host embedded video tutorials and articles covering interview fundamentals, including the 5 Cs framework and body language. |
| BR-02.5 | The content library must be fully searchable with filters for type, industry, career level, and topic, including autocomplete. |

---

### BR-03: Interactive Practice Tools

| ID | Business Requirement |
|----|---------------------|
| BR-03.1 | The platform must offer on-demand AI mock interview sessions that ask adaptive questions and record the candidate's audio and/or video response. |
| BR-03.2 | AI mock interviews must adapt difficulty and question type based on the candidate's profile (target role, industry) and past performance data. |
| BR-03.3 | The platform must enable candidates to schedule 1-on-1 live mock interview sessions with verified expert mentors. |
| BR-03.4 | The booking system must handle expert availability management, session confirmations, reminders, and cancellations. |
| BR-03.5 | Candidates must be able to record self-practice responses to any question in the bank and replay them with playback markers for pacing and tone. |
| BR-03.6 | The platform must send email and in-app notifications for session booking confirmations, reminders (24h and 1h before), and cancellation alerts. |

---

### BR-04: Evaluation & Feedback

| ID | Business Requirement |
|----|---------------------|
| BR-04.1 | After each AI mock interview session, the platform must provide an automated score covering: speech rate, filler word count, keyword relevance, and an overall clarity rating. |
| BR-04.2 | Human experts must be able to complete a structured evaluation rubric for each live mock session, covering communication, competence, and confidence. |
| BR-04.3 | All session recordings (AI and human-led), AI scores, and expert feedback must be archived and accessible to the candidate from a Feedback Archive. |
| BR-04.4 | Candidates must be able to replay past sessions and review annotated comments alongside the recording. |
| BR-04.5 | The platform must allow candidates to track improvement trends across multiple sessions over time. |

---

### BR-05: Technical & Non-Functional Business Requirements

| ID | Business Requirement |
|----|---------------------|
| BR-05.1 | The platform must support browser-native video sessions via WebRTC without requiring candidates to install software. |
| BR-05.2 | All spoken audio from mock sessions must be transcribed to text for AI scoring and archiving. |
| BR-05.3 | All candidate personal data (video, audio, transcripts, profiles) must be encrypted at rest and in transit. |
| BR-05.4 | The platform must comply with GDPR and CCPA, including a clearly documented data retention and deletion policy. |
| BR-05.5 | Recording consent must be explicitly obtained from all parties before any session recording begins. |
| BR-05.6 | The platform must be fully usable on desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers without a native app. |
| BR-05.7 | The platform must meet WCAG 2.1 AA accessibility standards. |

---

## 7. Out of Scope (v1)

- Native iOS or Android mobile applications
- Monetization / payment processing (to be resolved — see OQ-01)
- Multi-language support
- LMS integrations (Canvas, Moodle)
- Enterprise / institutional multi-tenant portals
- Live group mock interview sessions (1-on-1 only in v1)
